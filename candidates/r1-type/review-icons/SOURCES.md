# Sources

The baseline geometry, licence notices and upstream snapshots were copied with the incoming icon set. Retained or revised adaptations are listed exhaustively below. No additional upstream geometry was fetched for this revision.

The exact package URL, version and integrity remain in `sources/hugeicons-free/upstream.json`. This is the free Stroke Rounded package only.

## Other geometry

The following 17 original constructions were retained or revised from the incoming set: clear-day, clear-night, partly-cloudy-day, partly-cloudy-night, cloudy, fog, drizzle, rain, heavy-rain, thunderstorm, snow, sleet, hail, wind, tornado, today and places. Cloud contours, precipitation arrangements, the snowflake and calendar were drawn directly on the 24-unit grid for this revision.

Nine additions were constructed directly on that same grid without copying upstream paths: bell, clock, play, pause, humidity, sunrise, sunset, umbrella and wintry-mix. Wintry mix reuses the revised cloud, rain angle and snowflake. Basic geometric conventions such as circles, a playback triangle and a calendar are not claims of exclusive ownership.

## Licence handling

`LICENSES.md` retains the complete ISC and MIT notices supplied with the baseline, including the additional MIT notice for inherited portions of Lucide. The original upstream licence files and package metadata remain unchanged under `sources/`. Keep the notices with redistributed output. Required legal notices are preserved verbatim.

No proprietary outlines, exported symbol files, traced reference paths or paid icon assets were used. No additional external references were needed for this revision.
