## Round 15

Lucide (ISC) and Hugeicons free Stroke Rounded (MIT) remain the inherited adaptation bases listed above. LICENSES.md is byte-identical to round 14. No new upstream path or external asset was fetched, copied, traced or exported. All new geometry is direct construction on the inherited grid. Never use Hugeicons Pro. Other sets shown in the supplied sheets are convention references only.

- **freezing-rain:** a > c > b. a is the selected detached, broad open ice taper plus a detached liquid streak. Its pointed terminal and opposite slopes separate it from ordinary parallel rain; it has no snow crystal or hail circle. c tests one shorter, broader central taper with two flanking liquid streaks: more parts, the same 16 px density and less emphasis on a long ice tip. b tests rain falling beside a contact-glaze ledge with a hanging ice stroke: lighter, but the ledge can suggest furniture. All three have at least three ink components, density at most 24% and minimum measured gap at least two raster pixels at 16 CSS px. Reference: references/1-freezing-sleet-mix.png; use the icicle convention and the liquid/solid separation contrast, without borrowing outlines. a remains a visual choice pending blind first-read naming.
- **partly-cloudy-night:** a > b. Both isolate the whole crescent above-left of the inherited cloud. a retains a larger visible crescent body; b is a smaller, tighter study with extra separation. The partly-cloudy moon is offset down 3.5 units from the precipitation moon to centre the complete icon. Cloud coordinates remain exactly those of its day partner. Reference: references/4-partly-cloudy.png; separate celestial silhouette and layered-cloud conventions only.
- **rain-night:** a > b. Same crescent paths as the corresponding partly-cloudy studies, with the shared vertical placement rule. Cloud and all three rain streaks remain exact. a keeps a separate moon component and open counter; b tests the smaller alternate. Reference: references/4-partly-cloudy.png.
- **snow-night:** a > b. Same crescent construction and cloud as rain-night. The original crystal moves right 0.25 and down 0.18 units; it is not scaled. The same alternate moon remains in b. Reference: references/4-partly-cloudy.png for the night silhouette and references/1-freezing-sleet-mix.png for snow/ice contrast.
- **snow:** a only. Original six-arm crystal moves down 0.35 units. Cloud and arm lengths remain exact. Reference: references/1-freezing-sleet-mix.png. The extra cloud gap improves separation, with the cap-crop limit below.
- **snow-day:** a only. Original crystal moves right 0.25 and down 0.18 units; all sun and cloud bytes remain exact. Reference: references/1-freezing-sleet-mix.png, with the established day construction in references/4-partly-cloudy.png.
